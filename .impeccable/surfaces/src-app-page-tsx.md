# Home (/)

Mode: Persuade, restrained. Visitors arrive with a link (matchers, contract clients, hiring managers), stay 30 to 60 s, and need to confirm the CV and know how to reach Eduardo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a small exhibition of shipped products. Each product hangs on the wall as a framed screen with an identical museum label: title, company and years, stack, a one-line note, the measured outcome. It refuses the developer-portfolio template (giant name hero, device mockups, alternating cards, tag clouds, "Let's Connect").

OWN-WORLD: The pinned pastel diagonal gradient is the gallery wall, in both themes. Neutrals are the level1 to level5 tokens. One reserved ink, cobalt, only on the primary action and the contact address underline. Wall text in Brygada 1918, labels in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Frames are a mat border with an offset soft shadow, never a device mockup. Labels share one anatomy everywhere.

STORY: The visitor reads one sentence of what he builds, sees the GTE screen with its 1M+ label, scans more works with numbers, confirms dates in the Experience list, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with name as h1, title, nav (Work, Experience, CV, Email, theme). Left 5/12: wall text sentence at display size, paragraph with companies and the two lead numbers, availability line, cobalt "Email me" and "Download CV (PDF)". Right 7/12: GTE framed, label below. On 390: wall text, action, then GTE.

FORM: Exhibition wall with museum labels, candidate 4 of 7. seed:16b547ace0d33fd4
